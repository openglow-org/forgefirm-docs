# Privacy

This page tells you what this documentation site collects when you read it,
and who receives it. 514 LLC d/b/a OpenGlow publishes the site.

The site has no accounts, no forms, and no comments. The site search runs in
your browser. Only Google Analytics, when you allow it, receives the words you
search for.

## Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages people
read. We use the results to improve the documentation.

Google Analytics starts only after you allow it in the cookie banner. If you
select **Reject**, the Google Analytics code does not load.

When Google Analytics runs, your browser sends this data to Google:

- The pages you read, and the page that sent you to this site.
- The words you type in the site search.
- How you use a page, for example scrolls and clicks on links to other sites.
- Your browser, device type, screen size, and language.
- Your IP address. Google uses it to estimate your approximate location.

Google Analytics sets two cookies in your browser, `_ga` and
`_ga_YE997D7M7J`. They let Google tell a return visit from a new visit. The
site has no accounts, so we cannot connect this data to a name.

Google processes this data under its own terms. Read
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## The cookie banner

The banner shows on your first visit. It has two choices:

- **Google Analytics**: the visit count above.
- **GitHub**: the star and fork counts of the repository in the page header.
  With this choice on, your browser gets them from `api.github.com`.

**Accept** turns on both choices. **Reject** turns off both choices.
**Manage settings** lets you select each one.

To change your choice, select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page.

Your choice and your color scheme stay in the local storage of your browser.
They do not leave your device.

## Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs the IP address of every visit for
security purposes, also when you are not signed in to GitHub. Read
[GitHub's note on Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Diagrams

A page that shows a diagram loads the Mermaid diagram program from
`unpkg.com`, a public server for open source code. Your browser contacts
`unpkg.com` for it, so `unpkg.com` receives your IP address.

## Links to other sites

Links on this site go to other sites, for example the community forum and
GitHub. Those sites have their own privacy terms.

## How to stop the tracking

- Select **Reject** in the cookie banner. If you already made a choice,
  select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page and clear
  **Google Analytics**.
- Install the
  [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
  It blocks Google Analytics on all sites.
- Clear the cookies and the site data of this site in your browser. This
  removes the Google Analytics cookies and your banner choice.
